Taste match, powered by Qloo

Ayma is an AI matchmaker. Pick two demo profiles: Qloo's taste graph scores how much their tastes overlap across music, film, food, TV, books, podcasts, games, travel, brands and nightlife, reads the shared Qloo tags that explain why they match and the audience each taste skews to, then suggests date ideas they'd both enjoy. All four profiles are synthetic demo people.

×
Each demo person's public "Taste" page (e.g. Music: Khruangbin, Bon Iver) is resolved to Qloo entities; per-domain overlap of their Qloo recommendations gives the score, the genre/theme tags their graphs share give the "why you match", a Qloo demographics call reads the audience each taste skews to, and a joint, tag-aligned Qloo query (both people's signals at once) ranks the date spots. Only public profile data is used.
