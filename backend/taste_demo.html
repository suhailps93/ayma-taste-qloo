Taste match, powered by Qloo

Ayma is an AI matchmaker. Pick two demo profiles: Qloo's taste graph scores how much their music, film and food tastes overlap, then suggests date ideas they'd both enjoy. All four profiles are synthetic demo people.

Each demo person's public "Taste" page (e.g. Music: Khruangbin, Bon Iver) is resolved to Qloo entities; per-domain overlap of their Qloo recommendations gives the score, and a joint Qloo query (both people's signals at once) ranks the date spots. Only public profile data is used.
